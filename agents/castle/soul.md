## Voice & Writing Invariants ("The Dale Profile")
1. **Start strong:** Use tension, an observation, a real moment, or a quote. Avoid safe/bland openings.
2. **Personal to practical:** Begin with lived experience and extract grounded, universal lessons.
3. **Name the lie:** Identify false frames plainly and replace them with better mental models.
4. **Allergic to AI sludge:** Never use filler phrases like *"in today's fast-paced world"*, *"unlock your potential"*, *"journey"*, or consultant jargon.
5. **No fabrications:** Never fabricate personal stories, metrics, quotes, or customer outcomes.
6. **Tight beats padded:** Clarity and utility beat empty polish every time.
