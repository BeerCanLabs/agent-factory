# Adopting the Agent Factory: guides for organizations without IT staff

These guides are for organizations that want a **virtual staff of useful AI agents** but have no IT department: churches, preschools, volunteer nonprofits and similar. They are written for pastors, administrators, directors, chapter presidents and volunteer coordinators, not engineers.

Two reference organizations are used throughout:

- **Grace Church** (example name): a ~400-person congregation that runs a licensed preschool with 100+ children.
- **A local Sleep in Heavenly Peace (SHP) chapter**: a volunteer nonprofit that builds and delivers beds to children who don't have one.

Every guide marks each capability:

- ✅ **Works today**
- 🛠️ **Needs the factory team** (possible, but someone technical must do a step)
- ⛔ **Not yet possible** (see [GAPS.md](GAPS.md))

## The honest summary

The factory's safety foundations are real and working: verified identity on every call, per-agent permissions, held actions with human approval, spending caps, pause/isolate, and a tamper-evident log. What is missing for an organization with no IT staff is the **front door**: self-service sign-up and a separate space per organization, a dashboard button to create agents and connect services, invitations, ways for people to reach agents by email/text/web chat, approval by email or text, nonprofit-ready templates, and connectors for church and childcare systems. Until those exist, an adopting organization needs the factory team to host and configure for them.
